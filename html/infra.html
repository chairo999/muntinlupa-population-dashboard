MWSS Water Supply — Maynilad Service Area

Muntinlupa City • Source: Maynilad, as of Oct. 2024 & 2023 data

Maynilad (Maynilad Water Services, Inc.) is the water provider assigned to service cities and municipalities from the West Zone of the Greater Metropolitan Manila Area (GMMA) on which the City of Muntinlupa is included. It is an agent and contractor of the Metropolitan Waterworks and Sewerage System (MWSS). It was formed in 1997 after the consortium of Benpres Holdings Corporation (now the Lopez Group of Companies) and Suez Lyonnaise de Eaux won the exclusive right to provide water and wastewater services in the West Zone of GMMA. In January 24, 2007, a consortium led by Metro Pacific Investments Corporation (MPIC) and DMCI Holdings, Inc. (DMCI) took over majority of the company.

Angat Dam is the main source of water for Metro Manila, supplying 90% of raw water requirements through the facilities of the MWSS. Laguna de Bay is another source of water for Maynilad, mainly serving Muntinlupa and Cavite.

Power — Meralco Electrification

Muntinlupa City • Source: Meralco – Utility Economics & CPDO, 2023

The City of Muntinlupa's sole electric provider is Meralco (Manila Electric Railroad and Light Company). Meralco is the largest private electric distribution utility company in the Philippines, serving 39 cities and 72 municipalities with over 7,000 sq. km. of franchise area. Although it covers only about 3% of the country's land area, it supplies approximately 55% of the Philippines' electricity output. As of 2024, Meralco has been operating for 121 years.
